# Proposed pricing experiments and unit economics

Version: 1.0.0. Date: 2026-10-16. Owner: Founder / Product + Operations. Status: proposed — pending D-GTM-01/03 and D-VAL-01; no approved price or payment evidence. Delivery: experiment/model assumptions, not a price list or billing implementation. [Commercial packaging](COMMERCIAL_PACKAGING.md) retains proposed Starter $19–29 and Pro $49–59 ranges for the same single-business scope. This document evaluates the combined **$19–59/month range as an experiment**, not validated willingness to pay.

## Offer and packaging hypothesis

One Owner, Organization and Brand; all seven MVP features, with source/revision/security safeguards included at every tested price. No Agency, team, store sync, downloadable exports, connected tools or unlimited brands. No approval/provenance/privacy safeguard is a premium upsell. Deliverable evidence/claim review is a separate D-GTM-02 proposal: include in an offer only after approval and working delivery, or explicitly label human-assisted prototype service. Do not collect payment for undisclosed unavailable software.

Before any offer: approve and display finite supported file/source sizes, generation/usage allowances, retention, support coverage, overage/stop behavior, provider/data disclosure, refund/cancellation terms and delivered feature list. Limits remain Engineering/commercial review items, not invented entitlements. Do not use “unlimited” until actual cost/control obligations are understood. A proposed Pro name does not establish a premium feature split. A single tested offer can be called a pilot rather than imply a production tier.

If $29 cannot support required delivery costs, pause and revise price/usage/service design before offers, rather than assuming volume will fix negative contribution. If higher prices fail, diagnose real task value, buyer and support burden; do not remove core safeguards to make a lower price viable. Grandfather or change existing customers only under disclosed terms and explicit consent; no surprise charge changes. Any discounts/incentives are recorded separately at actual net price and cannot validate full-price demand.

The first test cannot simultaneously validate $19, $49, $59, Pro differentiation, annual retention or all e-commerce segments. Price tests require budget/term/limits approval, not merely a range in a document. Payment processing may be manual only if approved secure commercial operations exist; no integration has been implemented by this task. No outreach, billing or charges are authorized here.

## Unit-economics measurement contract

Report per business per comparable paid service month and original cohort, with cash receipts and recognized service-period revenue separately. Recognize net earned revenue after discount/refund/credit allocation, excluding collected taxes; annual prepayment cannot be treated as one month’s recurring revenue. Report unsettled/failed payments and refunds explicitly. Do not derive margin from list price when actual net revenue is lower.

- **CM1 (recurring contribution before customer support)** = net period revenue − payment fees − model/embedding/processing charges − variable storage/egress/queue/scanner/compute usage and other direct delivery costs.
- **CM2 (recurring contribution after support)** = CM1 − recurring customer-support/operations minutes × approved fully loaded hourly cost / 60. Contribution margin % = contribution / net period revenue; zero revenue → undefined percentage, not 100%.
- **First-period contribution** = CM2 − direct onboarding/import assistance cost and any incremental account provisioning expense. Report one-time setup and research assistance separately; do not erase it because Founder time is unpaid. Avoid double counting the same minutes as support and onboarding.
- **Allocated operating result** = total cohort contribution − allocated fixed hosting/database/monitoring/security costs. Base database/object/platform commitments are fixed/step costs, not falsely zero because marginal usage is small. Staff product development/research/sales cost is outside CM2 but tracked separately in runway/acquisition accounts.
- **Experimental acquisition cost** = attributable channel spend plus acquisition/sales hours at approved loaded cost / new real payers in that channel. Interview honoraria/research expense reported separately, with an additional all-in acquisition sensitivity. Zero payers → no finite CAC. Do not use payback = CAC/CM2 when CM2≤0, and do not claim stable LTV from a 90-day/ten-customer pilot.

Meter actual successful/failed/retried provider calls, input/output tokens where available, imported bytes/pages, scan/parse compute, object/egress usage, support/onboarding minutes and refunds. Provider minimums, credits and fixed fees need a transparent allocation rule. Keep actual vendor invoices/rate dates and estimates distinct. Content-minimized tenant/operation IDs join usage and payments in restricted financial analytics, not raw prompts or public customer invoices. Approval/retention requirements still apply.

## Illustrative sensitivity model — invented assumptions, not vendor quotes or forecasts

Assume zero refunds/taxes for this illustration only; actual model must replace them. Hypothetical transaction fee is **3% of price + $0.30** per monthly payment, not a verified processor tariff. Support labor assumption is **$30/hour**, not a market salary claim. The table describes usage profiles, not different product entitlements or planned price discrimination.

| Assumed monthly usage profile | AI/processing | Other variable operations | Recurring support | Total before payment fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | $2.50 | $1.00 | 5 min = $2.50 | $6.00 |
| Typical hypothetical | $3.00 | $1.50 | 15 min = $7.50 | $12.00 |
| Heavy | $12.00 | $3.00 | 45 min = $22.50 | $37.50 |

Illustrative CM2 = `0.97 × monthly price − 0.30 − profile cost`. Percentages use net revenue equal to price only in this no-refund example.

| Price experiment | Light CM2 / margin | Typical CM2 / margin | Heavy CM2 / margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $19 | $12.13 / 63.8% | $6.13 / 32.3% | −$19.37 / −101.9% |
| $29 | $21.83 / 75.3% | $15.83 / 54.6% | −$9.67 / −33.3% |
| $49 | $41.23 / 84.1% | $35.23 / 71.9% | $9.73 / 19.9% |
| $59 | $50.93 / 86.3% | $44.93 / 76.2% | $19.43 / 32.9% |

Interpretation: the proposed range is plausible only under observed delivery costs and actual demand. $19 is fragile under the typical assumptions and $29 loses money on the heavy profile; even $59 misses a proposed 50% CM2 goal for heavy support. This is a sensitivity finding from invented inputs, not a recommendation to charge $59 or evidence anyone will pay. If initial onboarding takes60 minutes at the assumed rate, an extra $30 makes typical $29 first-period contribution **−$14.17**, before acquisition and allocated fixed costs. Recovering it depends on real renewal and recurring contribution, not an assumed lifetime.

For net revenue P, fee fraction r, fixed transaction fee f and other variable costs C: CM2=`P(1−r)−f−C`; a target margin m requires `C ≤ P(1−r−m)−f`. At P=$29/r=3%/f=$0.30/m=50%, the hypothetical remaining budget is **$13.33**. With positive average CM2, fixed monthly cost coverage needs at least `ceil(fixed costs / average CM2)` retained payer-months; step costs and capacity changes need recalculation. No fixed infrastructure dollar estimate or break-even customer count is claimed without a real budget.

## Proposed economics gates and reporting

D-GTM-03 proposes: positive recurring CM2 in ≥8/10 fully costed original accounts and cohort CM2/net revenue ≥50% over a mature comparable service period, with first-period onboarding, CM1, costs and allocated result separately disclosed. Report median/tail usage and support, not only cohort average. Missing cost data → insufficient evidence, never a zero-cost assumption. Reconcile to invoices/receipts and log time weekly; review actual price/cost assumptions before every cohort.

Continue to another bounded cohort only with payment/use/renewal and benchmark gates, not margin alone. Mixed margin/demand → one approved price/usage/support/workflow retest with unchanged safeguards. Stop scaling an offer whose recurring contribution remains ≤0 after that bounded retest; safety/inability to fulfill stops selling immediately. A research subsidy must be explicit and time/budget capped. Existing MARKET_VALIDATION_PLAN stop thresholds remain in force. D-GTM-01/03 and commercial/security/privacy approvals are pending; all numerical targets here are proposed.

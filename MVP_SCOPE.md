# AI Brand OS — Canonical MVP Scope

Effective: 2026-10-13. Status: scope decision and requirements baseline, not a delivery report.

This is `docs/MVP_SCOPE.md` in the project, located at this docs repository’s root. It is the canonical matrix for MVP inclusion under the [document authority hierarchy](DOCUMENTATION_GOVERNANCE.md#authority-and-ownership); changes to approved strategy or scope require the responsible approver. Bibles, discovery hypotheses, PRDs, service specifications, domain concepts, API designs, and `schema.prisma` must conform to it. Design approval, schema declarations, and historical “Done” entries do not prove working software; implementation status requires separate runtime evidence.

The MVP tests whether a person values AI that retains approved business understanding. One account owns one Organization and one Brand. Additional client Organizations, brand switching, invitations, shared access, and delegated roles are excluded. Membership tables and role enum values support tenant ownership and future compatibility; application services must enforce the single-account boundary.

## Operational boundaries

- A source is one submitted Website URL (one page, no recursive crawl) or one PDF, DOCX, Markdown, or Plain Text file. Repeated sequential imports are allowed; one job may be in flight per Brand. Batch imports, multi-document reasoning, connected repositories, and scheduled sync are later-phase capabilities.
- Gate 1 validates eligibility. `AutoApproved` is a legacy governance label for a candidate eligible for review, not authoritative knowledge. Pending and rejected candidates may be stored as provisional/audit artifacts; neither may be retrieved as approved business context.
- Gate 2 requires explicit Owner confirmation or an explicit manual save. Edited candidates must pass validation again. Only then may Knowledge Management atomically write APPROVED_KNOWLEDGE, current business state, KnowledgeIndex, version lineage, approving user and timestamp. Context Engine and Brief read only approved state. Rejected/deleted candidates cannot replace previously approved facts.
- The Brief is a deterministic text view of approved Organization, Brand Profile, Brand Voice, Products/Services, Audience, and optional Competitor/Business Goal fields. Omit missing/unapproved facts. It does not require AI generation, an approval queue for the summary, downloads, or ExportJob. Workspace responses remain AI drafts; copying a response does not approve it as business knowledge.
- Content generation means requests inside the Workspace, including captions, posts, emails, descriptions, and copy. Persistent Content/Campaign/Asset/Persona/Channel modules are excluded. Feedback does not automatically alter knowledge, tune models, or implement learning workflows.
- Authentication, tenant isolation, file storage for imports, pipeline processing, audit/versioning, and operational telemetry are supporting dependencies. Platform module lists do not add billing, notifications, standalone Knowledge Graph infrastructure, or user analytics dashboards to MVP. Knowledge relationships may use the existing relational/artifact representation.

## Later phases and entry gates

Phase numbers describe sequence, not committed dates or delivery promises. Phase 2 begins only after MVP value and retention signals justify expansion; separate requirements must approve exports, multiple brands/Organizations, and team workflows. Phase 3 introduces validated marketing intelligence modules, persistent content/campaign/assets/personas, publishing/calendar and analytics as separately approved slices. Phase 4 introduces advanced integrations (CRM, connected Google Docs/Notion, public APIs), scheduled/batch sync, external actions, provider expansion, agents and automation. Phase 5 explores enterprise and cross-functional intelligence. Other deferred service optimizations require explicit phase assignment before implementation; they are not implicit MVP requirements.

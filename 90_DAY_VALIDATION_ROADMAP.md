# Proposed 90-day validation roadmap

Version: 1.0.0. Date: 2026-10-16. Owner: Founder / Product, with Engineering, Security/privacy and Operations. Status: proposed — pending D-GTM-01/02/03 and existing D-VAL/D-COMP gates. Day1 is a future authorized experiment start, not today or an invented launch date. No Founder approval, customers, payment, feature delivery or results are recorded by this roadmap.

## Goal and dependencies

Test whether a narrowly defined small e-commerce Owner will pay for, repeatedly use and renew a reviewed-facts marketing workflow that beats a competent native AI Project containing identical source information. [GO_TO_MARKET_STRATEGY.md](GO_TO_MARKET_STRATEGY.md) defines ICP, seven-step workflow, scope gaps, benchmark and retention; [PRICING_EXPERIMENTS.md](PRICING_EXPERIMENTS.md) defines the proposed $29 first offer inside the unvalidated $19–59 range, costs and economics gates.

Prerequisites: explicit segment/sampling/offer/protocol approval; known delivered feature list; working consent/instrumentation/evidence storage; critical security/retention/provider gates; Product/Engineering sign-off; source/claim UX decision D-GTM-02. Existing customer-validation execution gates remain effective. Coding progress or an approved strategy is not a passed customer gate. No work is assumed to finish merely because a calendar window is listed.

A calendar reality: a payer admitted on day56 is not mature through access+37 until **day93**. At day90, only payers admitted by day53 can have complete 37-day follow-up. Show mature/pending counts; complete the remaining three days (or longer for later authorized starts) before making an all-ten renewal decision. Do not shorten renewal windows, exclude late payers or claim completed retention to fit90 days. If pilot readiness/recruitment is delayed, record revised dates prospectively; day90 may legitimately end with insufficient evidence. The original six-week acquisition cap still needs an explicit revised start, not an endless rolling window.

## Proposed decision scorecard

Thresholds are proposed D-VAL-01/D-GTM-03 decisions, not statistical claims, forecasts or preapproved authorizations. Gate definitions in linked contracts prevail; do not average away a failure in safety or factual quality.

| Dimension | Continue signal | Pivot / stop / pause handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem / segment | ≥12/20 behavioral pain cases | 5–11 pivot; ≤4 stop; incomplete sample pauses empirical conclusion |
| Payment | ≥10 delivered-service payers among first40 equivalent offers, with independence mix | 4–9 pivot; ≤3 after completed40-offer test stop offer/segment; incomplete recruitment is insufficient evidence and channel/budget review |
| Onboarding/use/renewal/switching | ≥8/10 readiness by start+7d; ≥6/10 repeat use in each weeks2/3; ≥6/10 week4 use; ≥6/10 settled renewals; ≥5/10 narrow task substitution | Mixed → specific bounded retest. Mature ≤3/10 week4 users AND ≤3/10 renewals → stop subscription proposition. Missing follow-up/telemetry → pause, not invented successes |
| Blinded value | Proposed ≥30% relative material-error reduction with no worse coverage/unsupported claims; ≥20% paired median editing-time reduction; consistency safety screen/non-regression; ≥6/10 business preference | Zero baseline errors makes reduction undefined; no false pass. Mixed or incumbent tie → narrower proposition/retest; no differentiated-value claim from style preference alone |
| Economics | Fully costed mature cohort CM2 margin≥50%, positive recurring CM2 in≥8/10; show onboarding and allocated fixed costs separately | Missing cost → pause; mixed → bounded offer/cost retest; recurring cohort contribution≤0 after retest → stop scaling offer, explicitly fund any further research |
| Safety / delivered scope | No unresolved critical release issue, truthful offer and user-authorized data use | Immediately stop affected intake/sales/processing on a critical issue or inability to deliver; security/approval cannot be traded for conversion |

Continue means consider the next bounded experiment, not declare PMF, commit Agency/integrations or raise prices automatically. After one approved bounded retest, unmet continue gates require explicit Founder pivot/stop disposition rather than more unbounded cycles. No customer messaging, charges or public launch is authorized by these documents.

## Minimum review packet and measurement QA

Weekly packet: funnel by channel/relationship, exact offer/term, pseudonymous business cohort, readiness vs activation/TTFV, real-task usage evidence, refunds/renewals, benchmark completion/failed outputs and cost/support ledger. Preserve counterexamples and raw evidence under access/retention policy, not public Git. Monthly/closing packet adds comparable-period revenue retention, cost tail, cohort CM2 and one-time support burden; never treat annual cash or unpaid Founder time as recurring profit.

QA before collection: duplicate offers/payments/events count once; two users from one business do not double the sample; missing consent/corrupt joins are counted as missing; cancelled/refunded businesses stay in original cohort; baseline gets identical corrections; blinding failures are recorded; incomplete day37 observations are pending. Rehearse calculations with fictional fixtures, labeled synthetic, never actual customers. No schema/model changes or experiments are executed by this roadmap.

## A — Provenance-backed Brand Brain

**Customer problem:** an Owner cannot easily tell which source supports a price/claim, who accepted it or why an answer used an old value. Source citations alone do not show a fact’s review and revision history. Evidence of this pain remains H-01/04/12, not assumed demand.

**Competitor gap hypothesis:** generic project-file workflows may require manual lineage tracking; exact field/revision governance is U in several reviewed products. However, specialist structured context and a public repository’s field-source claims already overlap A. See [matrix](COMPETITIVE_STRATEGY.md#competitor-capability-matrix--knowledge-and-control). No claim that other vendors cannot support the workflow.

**Minimum implementation:** reuse [provenance](KNOWLEDGE_PROVENANCE.md) and [learning lifecycle](KNOWLEDGE_LEARNING_LIFECYCLE.md): material fact keys with MANUAL/IMPORTED/DERIVED origin and actual source/location or explicit unavailable marker; author/approver/verification metadata; current-vs-proposed diff; Owner review/edit/reject; immutable snapshot + revision; explain field history and restore through preview/new revision. Revalidate evidence/tenant/head atomically; pin revision during generation. A generated answer’s citation is not sufficient evidence of this complete contract. No new Workspace Context Inspector is made MVP by this proposal.

**Supporting infrastructure:** NestJS Knowledge Management/Context modules; existing PostgreSQL/Prisma Artifact/BrandBrainRevision/head/projections; private source storage; reference-validation/retention gateway; authenticated same-tenant reads/mutations and content-minimized audit. Keep candidate and approved state separate. Use [lean deployment ADR](domain/09_LEAN_MVP_ARCHITECTURE_ADR.md); Qdrant, Go or a new provenance database is unnecessary by default.

**Quantifiable benefit hypothesis / EXP-DIFF-A:** in paired comparable correction/source-check tasks, reduce median time to locate evidence and safely publish a correction by **≥20%** against each participant’s actual incumbent workflow, without increasing independently adjudicated factual errors. Technical gate: **100%** of published material facts resolve valid origin/revision metadata and required evidence or an allowed explicit manual/unavailable-location representation; this is traceability integrity, not 100% truth. Report fact count, completion failures, timing dispersion and error opportunities.

**Validation experiment:** extend EXP-04 with ten consented pilot businesses, counterbalanced task order and equivalent source access; compare correction, source inspection and restore tasks using their current ChatGPT/Claude workflow, and an available specialist where entitlements allow. Record exact product/plan/date/settings and assistance. Unavailable specialist trials remain U and cannot be counted as a win. Seed known wrong/current values with fictional fixtures for safety tests; separately adjudicate real-task facts against dated evidence. Thresholds are proposed, pending D-COMP-02, not observed results or statistically representative evidence.

**Status and limits:** existing MVP requirements; benefit/positioning is proposed. Schema and history can be copied; a small ontology is not a proprietary moat. Durable trust may emerge from good evidence UX, reliable revision integrity, measured outcomes and customer operations, but only continued use/renewal demonstrates value. Approval can still bless a false source; no certification or factual guarantee follows.

## B — Freshness and contradiction detection

**Customer problem:** current prices, offers and policy claims drift; an old source may conflict with a new correction. The cost of catching the error may exceed the generation time saved. Its incidence and value remain H-04/08 hypotheses.

**Competitor gap hypothesis:** distinguish business-fact due dates and explicit conflicting-value review from a brand-style violation or automatic source connector. Existing vendor freshness/conflict features overlap parts of B; matrix U cells do not establish a gap. Test whether our focused workflow is more understandable and less costly to operate for one Owner.

**Minimum MVP implementation:** compare candidates by stable entity/field keys and normalized types against the pinned approved revision; flag differing material values and unresolved entity matches for human review. Show both sources/values/dates; never infer “newest wins.” Explicit verification history and missing/unknown age labels are required. Stale field thresholds remain D-LEARN-01; until approved, show age/unknown without fabricated stale percentages. Fuzzy semantic contradictions are not promised solved by deterministic equality checks. Flags do not automatically alter completeness/readiness or business facts.

**Minimum later implementation:** Phase 2 adds an explicit versioned verification policy, due-list and bounded reminders, plus evaluated contradiction suggestions kept as candidates. Phase 4 source monitoring/connectors compare content digests against retained evidence and emit source-change candidates, never silent replacements. Source change does not itself establish that a business fact changed; absence of change does not prove freshness. Owner must approve each fact update.

**Supporting infrastructure:** existing revision/evidence keys and review queue for MVP. Later due-list may derive from verification timestamps + versioned policy; persist reminder/job state only when required, via the reviewed PostgreSQL queue. Monitored acquisition needs SSRF-safe fetch, quotas, authorized source connectors, checkpoint/retry and retention policies. External model-based suggestions require the provider/security contract and evaluation, not a second model/database by assumption.

**Quantifiable benefit hypothesis / EXP-DIFF-B:** on a declared corpus of 40 fictional field-change cases, detect **≥90%** of labeled same-field conflicts and flag **≤10%** of labeled nonconflicting cases (report denominators and unresolved cases); do not conflate conflict detection with truth adjudication. On paired real-task opportunities, target **≥30% relative reduction** in repeated adjudicated wrong-fact outputs after reviewed corrections, with no increased unrelated-error rate. Use the learning lifecycle’s zero-baseline and uncertainty rules. These are proposed evaluation thresholds, not delivered performance or approved freshness policy.

**Validation experiment:** balance conflict/nonconflict cases (20 each), include currencies/units/time validity, aliases, missing dates and ambiguous entities; use two reviewers with a resolution log and an explicit labeling rubric. Keep current reviewed-source cases separate from later monitored-source cases. During pilot follow-up, compare pinned old/new revisions with the same model/prompt/task setup and record reminder fatigue, time spent verifying and failures. A corpus pass does not validate production precision or retention causality.

**Status and limits:** review-time conflict/verification warning subset is specified MVP; reminders, richer suggestions and monitoring are proposals as phased above. Staleness is partly policy judgment, evidence may be dishonest, semantic contradictions are difficult, and vendors can add equivalent checks. Never market “always current,” comprehensive detection or autonomous verified learning.

## C — Model-independent knowledge portability

**Customer problem:** a business uses multiple AI tools and repeats briefing, loses source/approval semantics on copy-paste, or becomes dependent on one provider’s context format. Validate actual repeated work/switching through H-09/10, not abstract dislike of lock-in.

**Competitor gap hypothesis:** a neutral evidence-preserving package might improve transfer beyond prose export or provider selection. Existing MCP brand delivery and open-source configurable memory mean cross-tool context itself is competitive baseline. Need to test whether preserving fact semantics/revision state adds meaningful benefit; do not claim that competitors are closed or cannot export.

**Minimum MVP implementation:** canonical provider-independent knowledge and a copyable approved Brief assembled from a pinned revision, with revision/as-of identity and limited-context labeling. Users manually transfer it; this does not preserve every machine-readable citation/approval constraint or update pasted copies. Replaceable internal AI adapters do not imply customer choice of every model or an external API entitlement.

**Minimum later implementation:** Phase 2 proposes a versioned, tenant-authorized package of selected approved fact keys/values, origins/evidence references, approval/verification metadata, revision identity, ontology/schema versions, generated-at and integrity digest. Define exclusions/redaction and external evidence access; exclude raw source bytes/secrets/history by default. Reimport is an unapproved candidate, not portable approval authority; account/approver IDs cannot grant permission in another tenant. Preserve approved originals and explicitly map ontology differences. Restore and import remain distinct operations. Phase 4 may expose scoped read-only API/MCP retrieval with short-lived authorization, revocation, audit, rate limits and safe provider disclosure; no write/auto-publish tool is implied.

**Supporting infrastructure:** MVP uses existing approved snapshot/Brief paths. Package design needs schema-versioned serialization/validation, access-controlled on-demand delivery, reference mapping, tamper detection and retention policy. Add queue/ExportJob only if separately approved background generation requires it; not by default. MCP/API adds credential/session scope, consumer access policies, replay/revocation and deletion propagation. Downloaded/pasted copies outside our control cannot be recalled; disclose this limitation before transfer.

**Quantifiable benefit hypothesis / EXP-DIFF-C:** on a declared fixture set of 30 material facts, **100%** of permitted values, types, fact keys, provenance and revision metadata survive a package round-trip; excluded fields are enumerated, not silently dropped. Across two independently reviewed provider/tool consumers, target **≥50%** lower median repeat-briefing time versus manual re-entry and **no increase** in adjudicated factual error rate on the fixed questions. Round-trip integrity is a technical gate; time/error benefit must be measured separately. Tool adapters may omit unsupported semantics only with explicit loss report; then lossless acceptance fails.

**Validation experiment:** first run a copy-Brief manual task comparison within current MVP; do not label it a machine-round-trip pass. After separate package/integration approval, test two consumers selected for actual pilot workflows, same approved facts and equivalent tasks, tenant-confusion/adversarial package tests, tampered hashes, old ontology, expired evidence and revoked access. Observe semantic loss, transfer effort, stale-copy errors and both successful/failed imports. Preregister settings and minimum effect under D-COMP-02; ten customers do not establish universal interoperability.

**Status and limits:** manual Brief reuse/internal neutrality are specified MVP. Machine interchange is Phase 2; connected API/MCP Phase 4. Open formats and adapters are replicable; portability intentionally reduces lock-in and cannot depend on trapping customers’ data. Source licensing, provider behavior and privacy may limit what can move. A digested file can be modified and rehashed; authenticity requires authenticated source verification, not trusting the file’s own hash. Approved in source tenant never means approved in target tenant.

Sequence: deliver existing A/B/C subsets reliably → run market/pilot and paired value tests → revise positioning from observed evidence → approve the smallest justified later slice. Do not bundle all three into a launch scope expansion. If incumbents meet the same outcomes with lower friction, narrow the use case or pivot under the existing [validation gates](MARKET_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#continue-pivot-stop-and-pause-gates), rather than asserting a moat. Record disconfirming results.

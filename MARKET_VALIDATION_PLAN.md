## Evidence capture and preregistration

Before recruitment, approve segment, owner, exact offer/price/term, experiment dates, channels, eligibility, script, metric definitions, thresholds, budget/time cap, support policy, exclusions and privacy/retention policy. Assign EXP and evidence IDs. Record deviations before seeing outcome comparisons; never lower thresholds retroactively. This document does not authorize outreach, charges or publication.

Use pseudonymous participant/business IDs, consent and restricted source locations; identify real participants to the research owner without exposing personal details in public Git. A record includes date, segment/geography, recruitment channel/relationship, role/budget authority, source artifact/transcript timestamps, observed behavior, direct quote vs researcher inference, disconfirming evidence, analyst and linked hypothesis. Payment records include actual amount/currency/term, receipt reference, capability/limits delivered, refund/cancellation and renewal status. Keep financial/PII records in approved restricted storage, linked by access-controlled evidence ID. No participant rows or results are prepopulated.

Predeclare staff/test/fraud/duplicate exclusions; one business is one unit. Friends, referrals and compensated interviewees are tagged, not silently pooled with independent demand. Interview honoraria do not buy product demand. Track all contacted, screened, eligible, invited, offered, declined, paid, refunded and evaluable counts; nonresponse is not conversion. Preserve failures and unavailable evidence. User deletion/privacy obligations take precedence over retaining raw research; report minimized aggregates and missingness under approved policy.

## EXP-01 — Behavioral customer discovery

Initial proposed target: **20–30 interviews**, one decision-capable Owner per independent business. First gate uses the first 20 completed eligible interviews; up to 10 additional interviews may clarify a predeclared uncertain segment, reported separately rather than selected to improve the original ratio. Proposed sampling for first 20: 10 small service businesses and 10 single-brand e-commerce businesses; if recruitment differs, disclose it and do not claim the planned balance was achieved. Recruit through at least two channels and seek both frequent AI users and businesses that tried and stopped. Freelancers qualify only for their own/one authorized business; multi-client agency needs are separate future research.

Use 30–45 minute interviews. Ask about the last 30 days and a concrete recent task before showing any pitch. Request optional redacted artifacts/screenshares, never confidential customer data. Code specific repeated briefing/correction incidents, actual frequency, consequence/time/workaround, existing spend, buying process, switching history and reasons to stay. Two reviewers resolve coding disagreements; mark self-reported vs directly observed separately. Do not count praise or hypothetical agreement as a pain incident.

Behavioral questions:

1. Walk me through the last marketing task you completed. What triggered it, which tools did you use, and where did the result go?
2. Show a redacted example if comfortable. What information did you supply, and what had to be supplied again on another occasion?
3. Tell me about the last output you could not use. What specifically was wrong, how did you discover it, and what did you do next?
4. When did that happen previously? What did it cost in time, money or missed work? How did you estimate that?
5. Where do you currently keep product facts and brand guidance? Who changes/checks them, and when were they last wrong or out of date?
6. What workaround have you actually tried? What did you stop using, and why?
7. What AI/marketing tools did the business pay for last month? Who approved the purchase, and which budget paid for it?
8. Describe the last software switch you made. What made it worth doing and what data/workflow did you leave behind?
9. Which tasks would you keep in your existing AI tool even if another tool helped? What would block uploading business information?
10. If this issue remained for six months, what would you do? What competing problem gets budget first?
11. After the behavioral section, show a neutral single-business MVP description: what would you expect it to do, and which parts do you not need or trust? Ask them to describe it back without supplying the category label.
12. If appropriate, offer a separately documented paid-pilot invitation with concrete delivered scope. Record accept/decline and actual subsequent action; do not ask “Would you pay?” and call agreement a sale.

## EXP-02 — Paid pilot targeting 10 paying businesses

Proposed acquisition window: six weeks after a deliverable, safe pilot is ready. Target **10 independent paying customers** from up to the first 40 eligible businesses receiving a real, equivalent offer. Include interview and non-interview recruits with relationship/channel tags; include at least five non-interview businesses among the target ten to reduce interview-rapport bias. Predeclare how offers are selected; avoid cherry-picking only enthusiasts. Log all offers and declines. If fewer than 40 are reached at deadline, acquisition evidence is incomplete; do not claim a failed conversion rate on an invented denominator.

Choose one precise pilot price/term within the [proposed Starter range](COMMERCIAL_PACKAGING.md) before offers, with documented delivered limits and cancellation/refund terms. Pro remains a separate price hypothesis; a ten-customer Starter pilot cannot validate Pro or a feature split. Do not run a small uncontrolled price comparison and declare an optimum. A separate sequential price test must use its own cohort/offer and report actual payment differences and confounders. Agency/multi-brand/team features cannot be sold as this pilot.

Count a paying customer only when a real non-staff business pays the stated price and receives access to the described delivered service; exclude free credits, reimbursed payments, employee payments, verbal pledges and unpaid invoices. Report gross payers, net non-refunded payers and refunds separately. A deposit for undelivered software measures deposit commitment, not delivered-product payment or retention. Manual invoicing/payment collection is possible only under approved commercial/security arrangements; no billing implementation is assumed. Stop selling if the offer cannot be fulfilled or foundational security gates fail. Assisted/concierge onboarding must be disclosed, timed and separated from unassisted MVP evidence.

## EXP-03 — Onboarding, repeated use, retention and switching

Follow each payer from first paid access (day 0) through day 37. Keep all ten original payers in denominators, including refund, cancellation and inactivity; do not replace churned customers with recruits. Report mature observations separately from pending follow-up. Activation/TTFV use their original onboarding starts under PRODUCT_METRICS.md, not an artificially restarted payment clock.

| Test                        | Observable measure / proposed criterion on a fully observed ten-payer cohort                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Onboarding completion       | At least 8/10 reach effective generation readiness within seven days of original onboarding start. Separately report seven-day QualifiedFirstValue activation; a partial Brief may activate without readiness. Record support minutes and assisted/unassisted cases.                                                                                                              |
| First value                 | Track the existing median ≤10m and p90 ≤15m goals, all start/abandonment/missingness counts and converter-only percentile limits. These are proposed goals, not small-sample proof or new readiness thresholds.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Repeated usage              | At least 6/10 have qualifying productive activity on two distinct days in each of weeks 2 and 3 (day 7–13, 14–20 after access). Activity = a presented usable grounded Workspace output or approved Brief used for a real task; automated/test requests, login and identical repeated copy clicks do not count. Capture task-use evidence separately from presentation telemetry. |
| Week-4 behavioral retention | At least 6/10 have one qualifying real-task activity in days 21–27. All original payers are denominator; “active among survivors” is not retention. Report day-30 status separately.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Paid renewal                | At least 6/10 explicitly pay/renew for a second comparable period by day 37, excluding refunded renewals. Billing attempts, auto-renew toggle and stated intention are not settled retained revenue. For a different approved term, preregister equivalent due/grace windows before enrollment.                                                                                   |
| Willingness to switch       | At least 5/10 voluntarily move one named recurring task from their pre-recorded incumbent workflow and complete it here in both weeks 3 and 4, supported by task diary/redacted artifact. Keeping the incumbent for other tasks is allowed; no full subscription cancellation is required. Measure this as narrow workflow substitution, not universal tool replacement.          |
| Value and quality           | Record baseline vs observed briefing/correction effort on comparable tasks, factual adjudication per the learning lifecycle, wrong-fact incidents and reasons for abandonment. Satisfaction ratings are supplementary, not truth or renewal.                                                                                                                                      |

For missing behavioral evidence, report confirmed successes / original cohort plus missing counts; do not assume success. If consent/instrumentation failures prevent an adequately observed cohort, pause the outcome decision and repair measurement; do not silently drop users. Operational/provider outage remains product experience, not an exclusion. Show small-sample counts and uncertainty; these thresholds are managerial learning gates, not population estimates or PMF certification. AI/provider cost, human support, storage and acquisition effort must be recorded alongside revenue; no CAC/LTV projection is justified by ten customers.

## EXP-04 — Comparative value and positioning

With consent, use two comparable real-task briefs per participant, approved business facts and their existing AI tool as baseline. Counterbalance task/tool order where feasible, record settings and human assistance, time repeated briefing/correction and blind-score factual/brand consistency using a fixed rubric. Predeclare a minimum worthwhile effect after baseline inspection and before comparative outcomes (D-VAL-02); no result or effect target is invented here. Document information/context advantages rather than attributing every difference to the product. Positioning tests measure unaided description and follow-through on a real task/offer, not agreement with our “Brand Intelligence” category. A feature gap or high external adoption is not proof of defensibility.

## Continue, pivot, stop and pause gates

All gates below are **proposed — pending Founder/Product approval**, not standing authorization to override existing engineering/security/customer-validation gates. Register experiment start/end, actual evidence and rationale in the decision register. Freeze thresholds before collection. Product leads analysis; Founder owns strategic continue/pivot/stop; Security/Operations can stop an unsafe path.

| Gate                                                                                 | Continue                                                                                                                                                            | Pivot / bounded retest                                                                                                                | Stop                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discovery, first 20 completed eligible interviews                                    | ≥12 show a concrete repeated briefing/correction incident within 30 days with an actual workaround and consequence; identify a coherent single-business job/segment | 5–11 meet the rule, or pain concentrated outside intended segment; revise segment/problem and preregister one fresh test              | ≤4 meet the rule after completing the planned sample: stop this problem/segment proposition rather than inventing automation demand                       |
| Paid acquisition, first 40 equivalent qualified offers within approved six-week test | ≥10 delivered-service payers meeting scope and independence rules, with ≥5 non-interview recruits among target ten; disclose gross/net refunds                      | 4–9 pay, or relationship/price/delivery confounds prevent interpretation; revise offer/channel and test a new cohort once             | ≤3 pay after a complete 40-offer test: stop this offer/segment; safety or inability to fulfill stops selling immediately                                  |
| Ten-payer mature follow-up                                                           | Meet all onboarding, repeated-use, week-4, renewal and narrow-switch criteria above; no unresolved critical safety/delivery issue                                   | Mixed results or any criterion missed without stop condition: diagnose the specific failure, bound one revised test; no broad rollout | ≤3/10 week-4 real-task users **and** ≤3/10 settled renewals after mature follow-up: stop this subscription proposition, even if early enthusiasm was high |

Stop/safety rules take precedence over continue. Insufficient sample, incomplete follow-up or unreliable instrumentation means **pause / insufficient evidence**, not a fabricated pass/fail. Failure to recruit after the agreed budget/time cap triggers channel/segment review, not endless extension. After one bounded retest, any unmet continue gate requires an explicit Founder pivot/stop decision; no automatic additional cycles. Passing gates authorizes consideration of a larger scoped experiment, not proof of PMF or automatic future-feature funding. Publish negative results, cohort boundaries and residual uncertainty.

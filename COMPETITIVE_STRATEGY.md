Version: 1.0.0. Research date: 2026-10-16. Owner: Product / Founder. Status: strategic proposal — pending D-COMP-01/02 review. ## Proposed positioning and claim boundaries

> AI Brand OS is a verified, portable source of truth for business knowledge that keeps AI-generated work accurate, consistent, and up to date.

This is the proposed strategic direction, not an established performance or availability claim. “Verified” means accountable Owner review with evidence and explicit verification dates, not guaranteed objective truth or certification. “Portable” currently means a planned copyable approved Brand Brief and provider-independent internal contracts; full machine-readable interchange and connected-tool delivery are later proposals. “Up to date” requires Owner maintenance and dated warnings; no MVP continuous source synchronization is promised. Accuracy/consistency improvements require paired task evidence and ongoing review; approved facts and citations do not eliminate hallucinations.

Use this narrower MVP-safe description until delivery/benefit evidence supports the full proposition: **“AI Brand OS is designed to help one business review, version and reuse approved facts in AI work, with a copyable approved Brand Brief.”** Do not publish the full present-tense promise as a delivered guarantee. No uniqueness, first/only, no-hallucination, automatic-freshness or proven-moat claim is supported.

## Research method and evidence legend

Primary vendor documentation/product/pricing pages and maintainer repositories were reviewed on 2026-10-16. Dates below are publication/update dates only when actually displayed; otherwise “undated; accessed 2026-10-16.” These are supplier-described capabilities, not independent tests. Announcements are not general availability; repository README claims are not audited implementations. Authenticated screens, negotiated entitlements, exact onboarding times and undocumented field semantics were not tested. No private account or paid demo was accessed.

Matrix labels: **D** = documented in fetched primary source; **V** = vendor product-page claim, availability/behavior not tested; **R** = maintainer README claim, not executed; **P** = partial/adjacent capability with stated limitation; **U** = unverified/not established by reviewed material, never proof of absence. **S** = our specification, not delivered. Source IDs link to the ledger. Do not add checkmarks treating all forms of provenance, governance or versioning as equivalent.

Definitions: field provenance = origin/evidence tied to an individual fact and revision, beyond an answer citation; knowledge versioning = inspect/restore approved business values, beyond generated-content history; governance = explicit knowledge approval, beyond permissions or creative review; freshness = dates/due checks or changed-source checks, beyond silently syncing data; portability = preserving knowledge semantics across tools/providers, beyond switching a model or copying prose. Multiple projects/brand voices do not prove agency-grade isolation or billing.

## Commodity baseline and candidate opportunity

Inference from this sample: persistent project context, document knowledge, brand voice, basic retrieval, source citations and configurable provider adapters are widely available building blocks. Structured product context, governance and cross-tool brand delivery are already offered by specialist vendors; they are not uncontested whitespace. [Jasper Product IQ](https://www.jasper.ai/product-iq) and [Frontify MCP](https://help.frontify.com/en/articles/14787214-frontify-mcp) directly weaken claims that competitors lack structure or portability. Public repositories further weaken “a Brand Brain is inherently a moat.” This is a sampled competitive assessment, not proof every capability is cheap/easy or identical across products.

The candidate opportunity is **an understandable, reliable sequence for a single business: see field evidence → approve a fact → inspect/restore its revision → identify stale/conflicting facts → reuse a pinned approved knowledge package across tools**. Competitive gap hypothesis: existing options may make that exact workflow cumbersome or commercially mismatched for our ICP. Many matrix cells remain U, so that gap requires hands-on comparison and customer evidence, not a declaration of absence. A mature specialist may already outperform us.

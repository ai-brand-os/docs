# Proposed initial go-to-market strategy

Version: 1.0.0. Date: 2026-10-16. Owner: Product / Founder. Status: proposed — pending D-GTM-01/02/03; no new Founder-approved segment, positioning or price. Delivery: plan, not a launched offer or customer result. Dependencies: [MVP scope](MVP_SCOPE.md), [competitive strategy](COMPETITIVE_STRATEGY.md), [validation plan](MARKET_VALIDATION_PLAN.md), [pricing experiments](PRICING_EXPERIMENTS.md) and [90-day roadmap](90_DAY_VALIDATION_ROADMAP.md).

## Beachhead hypothesis

Test small, single-brand e-commerce businesses whose Owner already uses AI for recurring marketing work and frequently changes product information. The proposed advantage is less factual rechecking and repeated briefing, not better generic prose or a unique category. No payment/retention evidence establishes this segment yet.

| ICP dimension | Proposed recruitment rule / reason |
| --- | --- |
| Business | One English-language direct-to-consumer store, one Brand/Organization and one decision-capable Owner; typically 1–10 staff and 5–50 active products, as sampling hypotheses, not product limits or measured market demographics |
| Behavior | At least weekly AI-assisted marketing work, plus at least one product/price/approved-claim change per week in the last month; verify recent examples, current tools and actual consequences |
| First task | One selected product or offer can be represented by one public page or one supported source document, plus explicit manual corrections; willing to review facts before generation |
| Buyer | Owner responsible for marketing accuracy and able to authorize a small software purchase; interview the person doing the recurring work, not just an interested observer |
| Trigger | A launch, price change, updated product specification or repeatedly corrected marketing claim; ask what actually happened, not whether the person likes the concept |
| Exclusions for this test | Agencies/multiple client brands, team approval chains, large catalog synchronization, essential Shopify/CRM integration, image/video generation, automated publishing, and claims requiring specialist regulated-domain verification. These are fit exclusions, not assertions about market size. |
| Research unit | One business, not each staff member, product or interview; tag prior relationship, channel, existing AI plan and assisted onboarding |

Proposed first cohort: 20 completed e-commerce interviews, optionally extending to 30 for separately reported uncertainty. This **would amend** EXP-01’s proposed 10-service/10-e-commerce mix if D-GTM-01 and D-VAL-01 are approved before recruitment. Until then, the existing plan remains the baseline. Do not pool a service-business sample into an “e-commerce validated” finding or silently change an experiment already running.

## Jobs to be done and three high-value scenarios

Core job: **When a product fact changes, help me turn reviewed information into usable marketing copy without repeatedly explaining the business or publishing an outdated claim.** Functional outcome is a correct, reusable product message; emotional outcome is confidence about what was checked; social outcome is a consistent store voice. These are hypotheses to probe through recent behavior and paid usage.

| Scenario | Trigger / desired deliverable | Proposed workflow and evidence of value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch one product | Owner needs a short launch email and product blurb | Import one product fact sheet/page, approve product/audience/voice information, generate the first launch email. Compare correctness and editing minutes with their incumbent AI project; no campaign-management module is implied. |
| Price or offer update | Price, currency, bundle contents or offer end date changes | Explicit manual correction or a new single-source import, review old/new facts, approve a revision, generate a promotional caption using the new revision. Measure stale-price occurrences and repeated corrections; no live store sync. |
| Update an approved claim | Material, size, compatibility or permitted wording changes | Review evidence and uncertain assertions, keep unsupported claims out of approved facts, draft an FAQ/product description. Measure invented/unsupported claim rate and human checking effort; Owner approval is not legal certification. |

Choose one initial deliverable per business; do not demand a complete catalog before value. Other marketing formats are existing Workspace text drafts, not new modules. The three scenarios prioritize experiments rather than add capabilities.

## Customer journey and seven-step core workflow

| Step | Customer action / product behavior | MVP fit and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover / qualify | See a concrete before/after product fact example and a truthful delivered-scope offer; confirm recurring pain, budget and single-brand fit | Proposed channel/message experiment. No outreach or published claim is authorized by this document. |
| 1. Import website and product information | Submit **one product/company page URL OR one supported file per job**. Fill missing brand/offer facts manually; another source uses a separate later job after resolving the prior review under D-IMP-02 | Existing single-source import proposal. “Website import” never means crawling the store, simultaneous files+URL, catalog sync or integration. Security limits and pending D-IMP choices apply. |
| 2. Extract structured knowledge | Pipeline returns eligible product/brand/audience fields with source/location, confidence basis and contradiction flags | Existing import extraction, not automatic approval or continuous learning. Unknown/ineligible fields remain incomplete. |
| 3. Review important facts | Owner confirms/edits/rejects material product/price/claim fields and supplies mandatory organization/brand/audience/manual voice fields | Existing review. Display conflict against approved values; a high score cannot bypass mandatory fields or approval. |
| 4. Persist Brand Brain | Atomic approved revision/head/projections and completeness/readiness snapshot; inspect field evidence/history | Existing provenance/versioning contracts. “Persistent” follows retention policy, not a promise that nothing is ever erased. |
| 5. Generate grounded deliverable | Ready Brand produces the selected marketing draft with a pinned approved revision; if incomplete, offer setup and labeled partial approved Brief rather than generic generation | Existing context-aware Workspace; proposed TTFV median ≤10m/p90 ≤15m under PRODUCT_METRICS. A first token/import completion is not activation. |
| 6. Show support and flag questionable claims | Proposed deliverable review shows material claim → approved fact/source reference, revision/as-of identity, and unsupported/conflicting/unknown-verification warning; Owner revises or removes questionable copy | **New proposed source/claim UX**, D-GTM-02. Current Workspace hides assembled context and defers Source Viewer/Context Inspector. Field provenance in Brand Brain is existing; an inline deliverable evidence/claim panel is not already committed or implemented. Full workflow trial needs explicit PRD amendment/review. A researcher may demonstrate a clearly labeled manual annotation prototype, never claim an automatic shipped checker. |
| 7. Reuse approved Brief | Copy a Brief built from the approved revision into an existing AI tool, with partial/as-of labeling and customer review | Existing MVP-07. Manual reuse only; no connected MCP/API, downloadable/background export, automatic updates of pasted copies or customer entitlement to every AI provider. |
| Return / renew | Revisit the same recurring task after a fact change, explicitly update/review, compare outcome, choose whether to renew | Measure genuine task use and settled renewal, not accumulated facts, stored history or login counts. |

Step 6 minimum proposal: use explicit approved fact references and deterministic missing-reference/staleness/conflict indicators; label semantic claim detection coverage unknown until evaluated. A citation can be wrong or incomplete. Free-form generated assertions without support must be flagged for review, not silently labeled verified. This may require a typed response-to-fact mapping contract and safe UI, but no schema change is authorized by this GTM plan. Raw system prompts/secrets remain hidden; tenant-bound sources use the security access gateway. If step 6 is not approved/delivered, sell only the delivered workflow and label the demonstrated annotation as assisted/prototype; do not promise the complete seven-step automation.

## Before / after value and positioning tests

Before hypothesis: the Owner re-pastes context, reconciles changed prices/claims across files and rechecks similar AI drafts. After hypothesis: one reviewed revision feeds repeat tasks; evidence helps the Owner find questionable assertions and reuse an approved Brief. Measure error rate and total checking/editing effort, including initial review/support. Storage and extra approval work may cost more than they save.

Test three messages without uniqueness or outcome guarantees:

- **Accuracy:** “Draft product marketing from facts you have reviewed.”
- **Change control:** “Review a changed product fact once, then reuse that approved version in new AI work.”
- **Reusable knowledge:** “Keep an approved product brief you can copy into the AI tools you already use.”

The broader Task 14 “verified, portable source of truth” remains proposed and qualified; do not imply automatic freshness, certified truth or full interoperability. In interviews, ask for unaided interpretation and actual next action. Test message follow-through with the same delivered offer; clicks/praise alone do not establish willingness to pay. Avoid underpowered winner declarations from ten customers.

## MVP prioritization and acquisition

P0 existing: secure one-source intake, typed extraction, review/manual corrections, provenance/versioning, business readiness, approved-context text draft, basic feedback, copyable approved Brief, authorization/deletion/security/recovery. These preserve all seven canonical features; prioritization does not remove safeguards. P0 proposed experiment addition: the limited claim/source review experience in step 6, only after D-GTM-02 approval and validation. P1 later: machine-readable package, due-list/reminders after separate Phase 2 approval. Phase 4: connectors, monitoring, MCP/API; Agency/multi-brand/team expansion remains separate Phase 2 scope.

Proposed acquisition motion: Founder-led behavioral discovery followed by a small paid pilot. Compare two tagged recruitment channels: permission-based introductions through small-store operators/advisers, and opt-in e-commerce/AI practitioner communities. Prepare a one-product example using fictional or consented data, short workflow demonstration and exact paid-offer page only when deliverable. Record contact/offer/decline/payment funnels, relationship bias, spend and Founder hours. No assumed CAC, traffic or channel superiority; stop channel tests at preregistered budgets. Do not collect customer private data from public communities or send messages under this document’s authority.

## Blinded comparison — EXP-GTM-01

**Question:** does the proposed workflow outperform a standard AI project containing the **same source information**, rather than merely giving our system better information? [ChatGPT Projects](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/projects) and [Claude Projects](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9517075-what-are-projects) document reusable project files/instructions (reviewed 2026-10-16). Choose the participant’s incumbent where feasible; record exact plan, model label, date, native features and availability. Do not use a blank chat as a weak control.

Proposed sample: ten consenting businesses × three scenario tasks × two arms = 60 primary outputs, yielding 30 paired tasks. These are planned counts, not results or independent population samples. Use a separate fictional fixture set for unsupported/outdated facts. Preregister rubric, task budgets, model settings and thresholds before output collection; a pilot is not a powered efficacy trial.

1. Freeze a source packet (page/document snapshots, dates, product facts, approved claims/forbidden claims, brand voice and Owner corrections) and an independently adjudicated answer key. Both arms receive identical available source information and the **same Owner-approved fact/correction sheet**, including updates at the same time. Do not give Brand OS exclusive “correct answers.” Keep test questions/answer rubric hidden from generation. Each arm’s preparation/review effort is timed separately.
2. Arm A uses the delivered Brand OS pipeline; Arm B uses a fresh native ChatGPT/Claude Project with the same packet and neutral task instructions, without custom external databases/plugins. Both get equal preparation budget, output-length/task constraints and allowed revision rounds. Use a documented competent project setup; record if native capabilities make the workflow better than expected. No investigator prompt tuning only for A.
3. Two subtests: **controlled generation**, both starting from identical reviewed information; **end-to-end workflow**, timing ingestion/setup/approval, source checking and one follow-up fact change. Include both in reporting; approval overhead is not free. Do not transfer learning from the first task into the second unfairly; counterbalance order/task variants and reset project state as needed.
4. Generation operators know which UI they use; full double blinding is impossible. Export drafts to identical plain formatting, strip product labels, randomize A/B labels and order, and conceal tool identity from two independent factual raters and the editing/preference assessor. Keep source references/evidence annotations in a separate matched-format review packet, not branding that reveals the arm. Ask raters to guess the arm and record blinding failures. Owner familiarity may unblind preference; report it.
5. Use the same underlying model/version and settings where verifiably available. Hosted native-project model behavior may not match API versions/settings; if not matchable, report a **whole-product comparison** and do not attribute gains solely to provenance. An optional separately approved model-controlled offline test can isolate architecture, but cannot substitute for the native-project baseline. Disable unrelated web/tools in both arms; record unavoidable differences.
6. Independent raters compare factual claims to dated evidence, not model self-assessment. Resolve disagreement with documented evidence and report inter-rater agreement/missingness. Blind editor uses the same answer key/style rubric and timed task to reach publishable copy. Preference is collected before revealing labels or price. Counterbalance paired-task order and account for business-level clustering; do not treat 30 tasks as 30 independent customers.

| Outcome | Operational measure | Proposed practical pass criterion (D-GTM-03, not approved/observed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Factual accuracy | Correct / adjudicable claims; report unsupported/unverifiable fraction of all claims, material-error rate, and required-fact coverage to prevent “winning” by omitting facts | ≥30% relative reduction in material factual errors versus baseline, no worse required-fact coverage and no higher unsupported-claim rate. If baseline errors=0, relative reduction undefined; report accuracy non-regression and insufficient evidence of reduction. |
| Editing time | Active editor minutes from first draft to accepted deliverable, including source checking, failed attempts and corrections; separately report wall time and total setup/amortized work | ≥20% lower median paired editing time with equal factual acceptance. Report initial setup plus recurring tasks and the break-even number of tasks, not only best-case steady state. |
| Consistency | Within each scenario’s paired output set, conflicting values / repeated opportunities for the same fact; also rate adherence to a fixed approved voice/claim rubric | Zero critical outdated price/forbidden-claim incidents in seeded checks and no higher inconsistency rate; seeded checks are a safety screen, not a production guarantee. |
| User preference | Blind A/B/tie choice for each pair; aggregate by business majority with ties preserved; collect reason before reveal | ≥6/10 businesses prefer A overall, while accuracy/time criteria also hold. Style preference alone cannot pass. |

Refusals, failures, timeouts and uneditable drafts remain failures/completion-rate observations. Cap task time prospectively; report censored time/failure count rather than dropping slow runs. No cherry-picking/regeneration of only weak outputs. Report counts, distributions, paired deltas, uncertainty and negative cases, not significance claims unsupported by sample size. Step 6 annotations must be generated equally for scored copy or evaluated separately as UI assistance; manual annotations cannot masquerade as automatic A output. Baseline providers’ privacy/data-use settings and participants’ source consent require Security/privacy review.

## Retention, economics and decisions

Use [MARKET_VALIDATION_PLAN EXP-03](MARKET_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#exp-03--onboarding-repeated-use-retention-and-switching): original ten payers denominator, onboarding starts unchanged; ≥8/10 seven-day readiness, ≥6/10 repeat-use on two days in each weeks2/3, ≥6/10 week4 real-task use, ≥6/10 settled second-period renewals, ≥5/10 narrow task substitution in weeks3/4. Report refund/cancellation/inactivity, mature vs pending cohorts, assisted vs unassisted and missing evidence. Extend with logo retention = original payers still subscribed/paid at renewal / original payers; revenue retention = original cohort’s net recurring renewal revenue / original cohort’s comparable net first-period revenue, excluding new customers/one-time charges and with refunds disclosed. Do not annualize ten customers into LTV.

[PRICING_EXPERIMENTS.md](PRICING_EXPERIMENTS.md) defines contribution margin, support/onboarding cost and actual-cash vs recognized-period revenue. Proposed commercial continuation additionally needs positive recurring contribution in at least 8/10 fully costed accounts and ≥50% cohort recurring contribution margin, reported with first-period onboarding and allocated fixed-cost views; these are proposed learning gates, not market benchmarks. No unpaid Founder support is “free.”

Continue only when existing discovery/paid/usage gates, practical comparison criteria and proposed economics gates pass without critical safety/delivery gaps. Mixed value/economics results → one preregistered smaller workflow/offer retest. Use the existing stop rules (≤4/20 concrete pain incidents; ≤3/40 payers on a completed offer test; or mature ≤3/10 week4 users AND ≤3/10 renewals). Additionally stop scaling this offer if recurring cohort contribution is ≤0 after a bounded cost/price retest; an isolated subsidized pilot can be explicitly research-funded, never presented as a viable subscription. Missing data/late follow-up/undelivered step6 → pause or narrow the claim, not fabricated success. Founder owns strategic disposition after evidence review; existing engineering/security gates cannot be waived through this proposal.

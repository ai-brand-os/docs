# Executive Summary

AI Brand OS aims to become the operating system for AI-powered marketing.

Instead of competing as another AI content generator, the platform will provide a persistent **Brand Brain** that remembers a company's identity, knowledge, tone, products, and goals.

The MVP validates a single hypothesis:

> Businesses are willing to pay for AI that permanently understands their brand and consistently generates marketing content.

---

# Problem Statement

Historical problem hypothesis: some target workflows lose business context; current Projects and specialist tools already offer persistent context (see COMPETITIVE_STRATEGY.md).

Businesses repeatedly explain:

* Brand identity
* Products
* Services
* Tone of voice
* Target audience
* Marketing goals

Whether target Owners still repeat context across conversations requires behavioral evidence; it is not a universal competitor limitation.

This leads to:

* inconsistent outputs
* wasted time
* duplicated work
* reduced trust in AI

---

# Core Hypothesis

If businesses configure their brand once,

and AI permanently remembers it,

then they will:

* trust AI outputs more
* generate content faster
* reduce repetitive work
* become paying subscribers

# MVP Scope

Canonical definition: [MVP_SCOPE.md](../MVP_SCOPE.md).

Included:

- Brand Brain creation and editing
- Single-source knowledge import (one URL or one supported file per job)
- Human review and approval
- Persistent approved business knowledge
- Context-aware AI Workspace (including content drafts, history, and response copying)
- Basic response feedback
- Copyable, approved Brand Brief

Authentication, tenant isolation, storage, and single-owner Organization setup are supporting dependencies, not additional product modules. MVP permits one Organization and one Brand per account; multiple Organizations must not bypass the multi-brand exclusion.

Excluded:

- Background file exports — Phase 2
- Conversation PDF/DOCX exports — Phase 2
- Multi-brand management, including multiple client Organizations — Phase 2
- Team collaboration, invitations, shared access, and delegated roles — Phase 2
- Advanced integrations, scheduled sync, and external tool actions — Phase 4
- Publishing, scheduling, analytics, dedicated campaigns, assets, and personas — Phase 3 or later
- Agents, automation, marketplace, public API, image/video generation — Phase 4 or later

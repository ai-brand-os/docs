## Evidence-status rules

- **Assumption:** an untested premise, including qualitative star scores and strategic interpretations.
- **Hypothesis:** a falsifiable proposition with a linked experiment and criterion, not a conclusion.
- **Supported within stated scope:** identifiable dated evidence meets a preregistered criterion for a named sample/offer/window, with limits and counterevidence. Use only after owner review; record evidence ID and scope.
- **Contradicted / inconclusive:** evidence fails or cannot resolve the criterion. Preserve it; do not silently replace the cohort or test.

Avoid bare Validated/Confirmed/Proven labels. If retained for historical reasons, label what was actually approved or observed, when, by whom, and for which revision/population. Product/design approval and deterministic data/schema validation are different from empirical customer validation. Existing strategic decision records are preserved, not reclassified as fabricated customer interviews.

Approval of this plan is approval to use a protocol, not a finding. Preserve contradicted assumptions and supersede with a linked new hypothesis rather than rewriting history.

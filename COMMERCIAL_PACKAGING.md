# AI Brand OS — Commercial Packaging

Effective proposal: 2026-10-13. Status: proposed packaging and pricing hypotheses; no customer payment evidence is recorded in this repository that validates these offers. This is not a launch price list, billing implementation, or promise of delivered functionality. [MVP_SCOPE.md](MVP_SCOPE.md) defines product inclusion; [KNOWLEDGE_PROVENANCE.md](KNOWLEDGE_PROVENANCE.md) and [DATA_LIFECYCLE.md](DATA_LIFECYCLE.md) define knowledge and deletion requirements.

## MVP customer and account unit

The MVP targets a business managing **one Brand within one Organization**, with one Owner using the product. Primary recruitment: small businesses and e-commerce businesses creating content regularly and experiencing inconsistent AI context. A freelancer, creator or agency employee may participate only for their own business or one authorized business under this boundary; the MVP does not solve their multi-client portfolio or team-coordination needs. Market size lists and competitor customer segments are context, not MVP entitlements.

One MVP account may access one Organization; that Organization has at most one Brand row during setup and one Brand when onboarding completes. No additional client Organizations, cross-Organization memberships, invites, shared logins as a team offering, brand switching or pooled portfolio are MVP product capabilities. Relational structures supporting future memberships do not override these limits.

## Multiple memberships versus an Agency workspace

The schema’s User↔OrganizationMember many-to-many shape can structurally represent several memberships. **MVP does not support a user belonging to or operating across several Organizations through normal product operations.** Signup creates the sole Owner membership; additional Organization creation, invitations, switching and membership grants are blocked. This is an application-service restriction enforced transactionally, not an assertion that the existing membership schema has a one-row constraint. Pre-existing multiple memberships require explicit reconciliation before enabling an MVP account; do not silently choose a tenant or expose a switcher.

A later single user may receive independently authorized memberships in multiple isolated business Organizations. That grants separate tenant-scoped access according to each business’s role and subscription; it does not establish agency client ownership, pooled entitlements, consolidated billing, a multi-client dashboard or permission to mix knowledge. Identity, data ownership and the party paying a bill are distinct.

A true Agency workspace is a proposed portfolio/control layer with explicit agency-to-client relationships, commercial entitlements and permission scopes. Client Organizations remain separate tenant roots by default. Merely adding a dropdown over GET /organizations is insufficient. Multiple client Organizations can initially each keep a single Brand; enabling several Brands inside one Organization is a different product/ontology migration. Brand.organizationId remains @unique in MVP and is removed only for a separately approved multi-brand-per-Organization design.

## Journeys and market validation

MVP journey: recruit a single-business Owner → explain proposed test offer and exact limits → create account and sole Organization/Brand → create/import/review Brand Brain → see versioned weighted completeness and separate mandatory-field readiness per PRODUCT_METRICS.md → use business-aware Workspace and copy approved Brief/response → assess repeat use and willingness to pay. No client-selection screen, team invitation or Agency checkout belongs to this journey. A payment experiment is separate commercial research and adds no automated billing feature by implication.

Future Agency journey: validate multi-client demand → agree client ownership/access and billing model → authorize/link separate clients → allocate bounded entitlements → select client/Brand with full context reset → manage only that client’s knowledge and work → explicitly revoke/transfer/offboard. This is a proposal, not current onboarding.

Interview statements, competitive prices, star-rated segment estimates, waitlists, willingness-to-pay claims and a proposed tier name are hypotheses, not payment evidence. Record any payment experiment’s actual paid amount/currency/term, exact delivered capabilities/limits, customer segment, cohort/time, refunds/cancellations and continued usage/renewal evidence in a linked research record before changing status to validated. Do not invent counts or treat one payment as proof of all segments, prices or renewals. No such linked evidence is supplied by the current docs. Any published/test offer must describe only delivered capabilities; these design documents do not prove delivery.

Research priorities: single-business activation and context value, then paid willingness and retention, then justified Starter/Pro differentiation. Agency multi-client/team needs remain later-phase assumptions; they cannot justify an MVP workaround through multiple Organizations. GTM labels such as Private Beta/Public Beta are cohort stages and do not automatically enable Product Roadmap Phase 2 functionality.

## Unresolved commercial decisions

- Which single-business cohort converts at which price, based on actual payments and retention.
- Whether Starter/Pro need distinct offers at all; measurable usage allowances and enforcement remain unset.
- Currency/taxes, payment collection method, trials/refunds, subscription terms and billing operations before a paid offer.
- Future agency/client legal billing and data ownership, client transfers and role/seat/client limits.
- Sequence and evidence gates for membership expansion, Agency portfolio, team access and multi-brand-per-Organization support.

All prices and entitlements remain proposed until supported by delivered product and scoped customer payment evidence. No current tier promises unsupported MVP functionality.
